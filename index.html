Help

Choose the care tag or material, then tap a stain to open a card. Look for the tag under the cushions or along the frame. Follow the maker’s instructions for your specific model first. The card is a household cleaning reference.

Use Resume card on the home screen to return to the last card. To open a shared card, choose Import code, enter a code such as CSR1-W-COF or select a card file, then check the preview and use the card.

Privacy

The app does not collect any data. Your last selection, card and check status are stored on your device. The app has no account, advertising or analytics. Cards and codes are created on your device. When you choose to share them or open a source, the selected app or site handles that action under its own privacy policy.

沙发去渍急救卡帮助

先选护理标签或材质，再点污渍打开卡片。标签通常在坐垫下面或底框附近。优先遵循该型号的原厂说明，卡片仅供日常清洁参考。

点首页「续看卡片」可返回最近一次的卡片。收到分享时，点「导入口令」，输入 CSR1-W-COF 之类的口令或选择护理卡文件，核对预览后打开卡片。
